Entry for the make.fun bounty **news-image-20261118**: sum up the
world's news of Wednesday 18 November 2026 in one image.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. No network, no clock, no randomness — two runs
write byte-identical output.

## The image

A 1024×1024 newspaper front page, dated **Wednesday 18 November
2026**, with the six stories that defined the day:

1. **US sinks five Iranian tankers as war escalates** — Iran hits
   ships near Hormuz and a US base in Jordan; drones target US radar
   in Bahrain.
2. **Ukraine hits Kerch Bridge a second night** — Russia strikes Kyiv
   bridges before Thursday's Trump–Putin–Xi summit in Shenzhen.
3. **UN warns of more cold deaths in Gaza** — 1.7m displaced face a
   cold winter; four nations urge Israel to open aid.
4. **COP31 enters its decisive week** — ministers in Antalya debate a
   144-item draft split over fossil fuels.
5. **Eisenkot asked to form Israel's government** — President Herzog
   passes over Netanyahu after a deadlocked election.
6. **France's cost-of-living protests roll on** — marches enter a
   fourth weekend as unions press the government over prices.

Every headline was checked against reports published on 18 November
2026 (see `SOURCES.md` in the build notes); figures that conflicted
across outlets were left out rather than guessed.
